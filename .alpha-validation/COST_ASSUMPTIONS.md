# Cost Assumptions

- **Optimistic**: 5bps slippage, 2bps brokerage
- **Institutional**: 10bps slippage, 5bps brokerage
- **Realistic Retail**: 15bps slippage, 10bps brokerage
- **Conservative**: 30bps slippage, 20bps brokerage

All scenarios include STT (10bps), Exchange charges (0.32bps), SEBI (0.1bps) and 18% GST on brokerage.
